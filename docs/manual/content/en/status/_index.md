The *Status* menu shows how the PBX is doing right now and what has happened: which phones and providers are registered, which calls are running, how busy the system is, what the logs say and who changed what. Nothing on these pages changes the configuration, so you can look around safely.

When something does not work, these pages are the first place to look; see also [Troubleshooting]({{< relref "/reference/troubleshooting.md" >}}).

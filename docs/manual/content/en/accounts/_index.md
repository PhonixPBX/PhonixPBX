The *Accounts* menu holds the two kinds of accounts the PBX talks to: the **phones** of your users and the **providers** (SIP or IAX trunks) that connect the PBX to the public telephone network.

A typical setup starts here: create a phone for every desk phone or softphone, add your provider account, give the provider [dial patterns]({{< relref "/accounts/providers.md#dial-patterns" >}}) for outgoing calls and [incoming routes]({{< relref "/accounts/providers.md#incoming-routes" >}}) for incoming calls, then apply the changes.

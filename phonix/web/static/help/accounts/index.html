Accounts

The Accounts menu holds the two kinds of accounts the PBX talks to: the phones of your users and the providers (SIP or IAX trunks) that connect the PBX to the public telephone network.

A typical setup starts here: create a phone for every desk phone or softphone, add your provider account, give the provider dial patterns for outgoing calls and incoming routes for incoming calls, then apply the changes.

Phones
SIP, IAX and external phones: numbers, passwords, voicemail, outgoing rules and how to register a device.

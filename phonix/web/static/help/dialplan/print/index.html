Dialplan

Overview (print)

Dialplan → Overview (print) collects the numbering plan of the PBX on one page, for example to hang up next to the reception or to check for gaps before adding numbers. Click Print to print it; the menu bars are left out of the printout.

The page shows the configuration as it is saved, including changes that have not been applied yet.

Internal numbers

All phones, call groups with a number, conference rooms and applications, sorted by number:

ColumnMeaning
NumberThe internal number.
TypePhone (SIP), Phone (IAX), Phone (external), Call group, Conference or Application.
NameThe caller ID name of a phone, or the name of the object.
DetailsThe description of a phone or application, the member numbers of a call group, or PIN protected for a conference room.
Publicyes (or the public name) when the number is reachable from outside.

IVR menus and business hours are not part of this list; see Dialplan → IVR Menus and Dialplan → Business Hours.

Feature codes

The codes for attended and blind transfer, call pickup and disconnect, and the call parking number with its slots, as set under Dialplan → Transfers & Parking.

Providers

For every provider, the outgoing dial patterns and the incoming routes as pattern → destination (all for routes without a pattern).
